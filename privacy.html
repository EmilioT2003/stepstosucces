Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains our commitment to protecting your privacy while using our platform.

Privacy-First Approach

We do not collect any personal data.

Steps to Success is designed with privacy as a fundamental principle. This static website operates without any data collection mechanisms, ensuring your complete privacy while browsing our content.

No Cookies Policy

This website does not use cookies.

  • No tracking cookies
  • No analytics cookies
  • No advertising cookies
  • No session cookies
  • No third-party cookies

You can browse our platform without any concern about cookie consent or data tracking.

No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information including:

  • Personal identification information
  • Email addresses
  • IP addresses
  • Browser information
  • Device information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics
  • Behavioral tracking data

No Third-Party Services

Our platform operates independently without integration of third-party services that might collect data:

  • No Google Analytics or similar tracking
  • No social media tracking pixels
  • No advertising networks
  • No external analytics services
  • No customer relationship management (CRM) systems

Static Website Technology

Steps to Success is built as a static website, which inherently provides strong privacy protection:

  • No server-side processing of user data
  • No database storing user information
  • No dynamic content generation based on user behavior
  • Content delivered directly from GitHub Pages

External Links

Our platform may contain links to external resources for reference purposes. Please note:

  • External websites have their own privacy policies
  • We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices
  • Review external privacy policies before providing information
  • External links are provided for informational purposes only

GitHub Pages Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect certain technical information as part of their hosting service:

  • Server logs for technical maintenance (GitHub's responsibility)
  • Basic security monitoring (GitHub's responsibility)
  • We do not have access to any GitHub server logs
  • Refer to GitHub's Privacy Policy for their practices

Contact and Questions

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices:

GitHub Issues: Submit a question via GitHub Issues

Please note that any communication through GitHub will be subject to GitHub's privacy policy and terms of service.

Policy Updates

Any changes to this privacy policy will be reflected in the repository and on this page. Given our no-data-collection approach, policy changes are unlikely but would be clearly communicated if they occur.
